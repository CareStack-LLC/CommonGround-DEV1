Glossary

Overall Compliance Score
A weighted metric combining exchange completion (35%), communication health (25%), financial compliance (25%), and GPS verification (15%).
Exchange Rate
The percentage of scheduled custody exchanges that were completed as planned.
Communication Health
A 0-100 score reflecting the quality of parent-to-parent messaging, including toxicity levels and ARIA intervention frequency.
ARIA Intervention
When the AI assistant detects potentially harmful language and suggests a constructive alternative before the message is sent.
GPS Verification
Location confirmation using the parent's mobile device at custody exchanges, recorded when they check in through the app.
ClearFund
CommonGround's financial tracking system for managing shared parenting expenses and child support payments.
