How This Data Was Collected
Custody time is determined from: exchange check-ins (highest confidence), completed custody exchanges, the agreed parenting schedule, and manual "With Me" confirmations. Days without any recorded data are marked as unrecorded.

Glossary

Custody Split
The percentage of time each parent has physical custody of the child(ren).
Variance
The difference between the agreed custody schedule and the actual time spent. Positive means more time than agreed; negative means less.
Exchange Compliance
The percentage of scheduled custody exchanges that were completed as planned.
GPS Verification
Location confirmation using the parent's mobile device to verify they were at the agreed exchange location.
Overnights
Nights where the child slept at a parent's residence, used to calculate custody time percentages.
Check-in
When a parent confirms their presence at a custody exchange via the CommonGround app.
