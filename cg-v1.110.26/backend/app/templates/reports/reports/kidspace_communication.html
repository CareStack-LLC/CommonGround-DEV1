Glossary

KidSpace
CommonGround's child-friendly communication hub for video calls, chats, and shared activities with approved contacts.
Circle Contact
An adult approved by both parents to communicate with the child through KidSpace. Contacts must be added and approved before communication is allowed.
Session
A single communication event in KidSpace — this can be a video call, chat conversation, or shared theater activity.
ARIA Monitoring
AI-powered safety monitoring that reviews KidSpace messages for concerning content and flags them for parental review.
Communication Window
The allowed hours during which KidSpace sessions can take place, as configured in the family settings.
Theater
A KidSpace feature where children and contacts can watch content or do activities together during a video session.
