Glossary

Custody Exchange
A scheduled transfer of the child(ren) from one parent to the other, at a designated time and location.
GPS Verification
Location confirmation using the parent's mobile device to verify they were at the agreed exchange location when they checked in.
Exchange Location Radius
The allowed distance from the agreed meeting point. Check-ins within this radius are marked as "verified."
Schedule Modification
A request by one parent to change a scheduled event, which may require approval from the other parent.
Completion Rate
The percentage of scheduled custody exchanges that were completed as planned, excluding cancelled exchanges.
Check-in
When a parent confirms their presence at a custody exchange via the CommonGround app, recording their GPS location and timestamp.
