# CommonGround — Conversion Copy Playbook

**Companion to [BRAND_GUIDE.md](./BRAND_GUIDE.md). Governs all marketing copy.**

Our reader is a stressed, often frightened parent (or a busy professional). We
convert by **lowering the temperature and showing the way out** — not by hyping.
Sharpen the calm voice: clearer, more specific, more benefit-led, less friction.
Never louder, never pushy, never fake.

---

## The one-line test

Every hero must let the reader answer three questions in five seconds:
**What is this? What do I get? What do I do next?** If a headline doesn't move
one of those forward, cut or rewrite it.

## Voice (unchanged from the brand guide)

Quietly modern · emotionally safe · court-credible. Child-first. Neutral
(Parent A / Parent B, "you / the other parent"). Empathetic. Plain language
(~8th grade). Gender-neutral. Short sentences win.

---

## Headlines

- **Lead with the outcome or the tension, not the tool.** "Co-parent without the
  fight" beats "AI-powered messaging." Name the feeling before the feature.
- Keep our ownable lines (e.g. *"The calm way to co-parent"*). Don't churn strong
  equity for novelty.
- One idea per headline. Put the payoff in the first four words.
- Specific > grand. "Every message, handoff, and payment — documented" beats
  "The complete co-parenting solution."

## Subheads (the money line)

Formula: **outcome first → how (named feature) → proof/So-that.**
> *You handle your kids. CommonGround handles the coordination — messaging,
> schedules, and expenses — and quietly documents all of it, court-ready.*

- Cut filler ("Our platform helps you to…"). Start on the verb or the benefit.
- ≤ 2 sentences. If it needs three, the headline is underperforming.

## CTAs

- **Action + outcome + low risk.** "Start free — no card needed" beats "Sign up."
- **Kill vague CTAs.** Replace "Learn more" / "See details" / "View more" with the
  specific next step: *See plans & pricing, Try the ARIA demo, Take the 2-min tour,
  Get court-ready today.*
- One clear **primary** CTA per section; everything else is visibly secondary.
- Repeat the primary CTA at hero, mid-page, and end. Don't make people scroll back.
- Put **risk-reversal microcopy** next to the button: *Free forever · No credit
  card · 2-minute setup · Cancel anytime · Your data stays yours.*

## Proof & specificity (honesty is non-negotiable)

- Prefer **concrete mechanisms** over adjectives: "SHA-256 verified exports,"
  "GPS-checked handoffs," "every message timestamped." These are true and they sell.
- **Never invent statistics, user counts, outcomes, or testimonials.** This is a
  court-credible legal product; a fabricated number is a liability, not a hook.
- Do **not** add new named quotes or families. Existing grant stories
  (4Ever Forward) and any `TODO(marketing)`/placeholder testimonials stay as-is —
  never fabricate around them, and don't attribute new claims to real people.
- If a stat already on the page is unverifiable, don't amplify it; prefer to reframe
  it as a described capability rather than a hard number. Flag, don't invent.
- Comparative/illustrative framings ("about the cost of one coffee a week") are
  fine when clearly illustrative and not stated as a measured fact.

## Friction reducers (worth real conversion points)

- State the **free path** early and everywhere. "$0 forever" is our best hook.
- Remove jargon that raises friction: prefer "start free" over "start your family
  file," "see plans" over "explore tiers."
- Answer the silent objection near the CTA: *Will the other parent have to join?
  Is my data private? Can I use it for court? How long to set up?*
- Reassure the anxious: no lock-in, no card, works on any device, private by default.

## Structure of a high-converting page

1. **Hero** — outcome headline + money subhead + primary CTA + risk-reversal + proof image.
2. **Problem → relief** — name the specific pain, then the specific fix (mechanism).
3. **How it works** — 3 steps, plain verbs.
4. **Proof / trust** — mechanisms, security, credibility (only true claims).
5. **Pricing clarity** — free tier first, no surprises.
6. **Objections / FAQ** — remove the last reasons not to act.
7. **Close** — restate the outcome + primary CTA + reassurance.

## Word-level do / don't

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| you, your kids, calm, documented, court-ready, free | leverage, seamless, revolutionary, best-in-class |
| specific verbs: rewrites, splits, logs, verifies | vague verbs: empowers, enables, facilitates |
| honest scarcity ("early access") | fake urgency ("only 3 spots left!") |
| "no credit card · cancel anytime" | buried or missing risk-reversal |

## Feature glossary (use exact names, consistently)

**ARIA** (message-tone AI) · **TimeBridge** (automated schedule) · **ClearFund**
(shared expenses) · **KidSpace / KidComs** (kids' video & chat) · **Silent Handoff**
(GPS-verified, contactless exchanges) · **Agreement Builder** · **Court-ready
exports** (SHA-256) · **Professional portal** · **My Circle** (approved contacts).
First mention on a page may add a 3–4 word gloss; after that, just the name.
