05 — Design Rationale

The Icon reads like a calm smile. Two parent figures (teal left, blue right) sit at the top — distinct, separate, but equal. Between them, a golden arch rises like a bridge of communication. Below it, a gold child figure anchors the composition, small but always the focal point. Together the shape forms a face at peace — the quiet relief families feel when conflict gives way to cooperation.

Typography: Georgia serif for the wordmark signals authority and warmth simultaneously — trusted by attorneys in courtrooms and readable by parents under stress. Common in bold navy grounds the name; Ground in teal gradient lifts it.

Gold (#F5A623) is used only for the child and the connecting arch — it always draws the eye to what matters most. Parents are teal and blue; the child is gold. Every time.
