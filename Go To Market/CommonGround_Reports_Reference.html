Data Sources — Where Every Report Pulls From

Every field in every report maps back to one of nine database tables. Understanding the source table for each data point determines how fresh the data is, whether it's real-time or aggregated, and what tier is needed to access it.

Source Table What It Stores Freshness Used In
messages
message_flags		 Every message sent between co-parents, plus ARIA's analysis of each — toxicity score, category, intervention type, parent's action (accepted/rejected/ignored) Real-time on send Reports 1, 2, 5, 6, 9, 12
custody_exchanges
custody_exchange_instances		 Scheduled exchanges, actual check-in timestamps, GPS coordinates at check-in, QR scan confirmation, on-time status (calculated against scheduled time ± 15 min) Real-time on check-in Reports 1, 2, 4, 6, 7, 8, 12
obligations
clearfund_expenses		 Court-ordered financial obligations, expense requests with receipts, approval status, payment records via Stripe, disputed amounts Real-time on submission Reports 1, 2, 3, 6, 7, 12
schedule_events
custody_periods		 All calendar events, planned custody blocks, actual vs. planned time deltas, holiday schedule, school year schedule Real-time Reports 4, 7, 8
agreements
agreement_versions		 SharedCare Agreement sections, version history with change tracking, dual-parent signature timestamps, agreement status On approval Reports 4, 6, 12
audit_logs Tamper-evident append-only log of every platform action. Each entry includes SHA-256 hash chained to the previous entry. Court chain-of-custody foundation. Real-time on every action Reports 6, 12 (primary)
court_events Scheduled hearings, mediation sessions, deadlines, GAL reports, modification filings — all linked to case On creation/update Reports 6, 7, 12
kidcoms_calls Video call logs, duration, participants, ARIA moderation flags, evidence clips (flagged calls), SHA-256 hashed recordings metadata Post-call processing Reports 2, 6, 12
professional_cases
case_assignments		 Attorney-case links, attorney role, access timestamps, report generation history, case intake data from ARIA Pro Intake Real-time Reports 7–12
★

SHA-256 chain-of-custody: Every record in audit_logs includes a cryptographic hash of its own content chained to the hash of the previous entry. This means any tampering with historical records is mathematically detectable. Reports that include this chain are admissible under Federal Rule of Evidence 902(14) as self-authenticating. Only Complete tier (parent) and all Professional tiers include SHA-256 verified exports.

Auto-generated on the 1st of each month. PDF exportable. Includes trend lines comparing this month to the prior two months. Designed for parents, therapists, and light attorney review — not court submission.

A clean, itemized financial statement covering all shared expenses for the month. Designed to be shared with an accountant, included in modification filings, or used to resolve disputes — not a full court package, but organized and exportable.

REPORT P-3 · CLEARFUND EXPENSE SUMMARY
Monthly Expense Summary
Itemized monthly statement of all child-related expenses tracked in ClearFund. Includes receipt references, approval status, and outstanding balance. Available on Plus and Complete.
Plus — $17.99/mo Auto · Monthly Custom Date Range
PDF export · No SHA-256
What's In It
  • Summary header: Period, both parent names, total in / total out, net balance
  • Expense line items: Date, category (medical/education/activity/other), description, amount, who submitted, split %, approval status

REPORT P-4 · CUSTODY ANALYTICS
Custody Analytics Report
Actual vs. planned parenting time analysis with exchange compliance history. On-demand or scheduled. Complete tier only. Designed to support modification filings, custody enforcement, and attorney case prep.
Complete — $34.99/mo On-Demand Auto · Quarterly
PDF export · SHA-256 optional
What's In It
  • Agreement vs. Reality: Ordered custody % per child vs. actual % by days/hours this period

ARIA Communication Report

REPORT P-5 · ARIA COMMUNICATION REPORT
ARIA Communication Report
Shows the complete ARIA intervention history with conflict trend analysis. Demonstrates the parent's good-faith effort to communicate constructively. Useful for attorneys showing a client's behavior pattern and court demonstration of co-operation.
Complete — $34.99/mo On-Demand
PDF export · SHA-256 included
What's In It
  • Total messages sent (period), by parent
  • Total ARIA flags by severity (green/yellow/orange/red)
  • Flag category breakdown: hostility · blame · passive-aggressive · profanity · controlling · dismissive
  • ARIA acceptance rate — how often this parent accepted the suggestion
  • Monthly trend: flag rate % over the report period
  • Conflict escalation events — any red-level flags with timestamp (not the message content)
  • Comparative behavior: your flag rate vs. co-parent flag rate (without showing their messages)
  • Most common intervention type (what ARIA most frequently helped with)
  • Constructive communication score: ratio of messages sent that required zero ARIA intervention
Data Sources

Format + Layout
  • 4 pages: Cover · Communication Health · Intervention Detail · Trend Analysis
  • Chart: Monthly stacked bar — green/yellow/orange/red flags over time
  • SHA-256 footer on every page — this report is court-admissible as behavioral evidence
  • Attorney-readable: designed to be handed to a lawyer, not just the parent

GPS Exchange Verification Report
GPS-verified record of every custody exchange in the period. The definitive answer to he-said/she-said disputes about pickups and drop-offs. Court-ready, SHA-256 signed, maps-integrated.
Solo+ — $99/mo On-Demand SHA-256 Verified

  • Scheduled time vs. GPS check-in time — delta in minutes
  • GPS coordinates at check-in — compared against agreed exchange location
