★

The highest uncontrolled risk is AWS and AI costs. A bug in the ARIA loop (calling the Claude API in an infinite loop) or misconfigured S3 permissions (public bucket getting scraped) can generate thousands of dollars in hours. Set billing alerts on AWS ($50 and $200/mo), Anthropic ($100/mo), and OpenAI ($50/mo) before any of these go live. This takes 10 minutes and has saved many startups from catastrophic surprise bills.
