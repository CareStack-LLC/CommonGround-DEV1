HOW TO REPORT BUGS
-------------------
  1. Use the feedback widget (bottom-right of every page) -- click "Report a Bug"
  2. Take a screenshot of the issue and upload it
  3. Describe what happened vs. what you expected
  4. Rate severity:
       [CRITICAL] Can't use the app
       [MAJOR]    Feature broken
       [MINOR]    Cosmetic/UX issue
  5. Report everything -- even small things like typos, slow loading, or confusing labels

WHAT TO LOOK FOR
-----------------

  [CRITICAL] -- report immediately
    - Can't log in
    - Data showing for the wrong family
    - Messages going to wrong person
    - Security issues (seeing other families' data)
    - App crashes / white screen
    - Payment or financial data errors

  [MAJOR]
    - Features that don't work (buttons do nothing)
    - ARIA not catching obvious hostile messages
    - Agreement sections missing or blank
    - Calendar showing wrong dates
    - Notifications not arriving
    - Profile changes not saving

  [MINOR]
    - Typos or grammar errors in the UI
    - Slow loading (note which page and how long)
    - Buttons in wrong position on mobile
    - Colors or fonts that look off
    - Confusing labels or instructions
    - Inconsistent behavior

  [UX FEEDBACK] -- not bugs, but just as valuable
    - "I didn't know where to find X"
    - "This took too many clicks"
    - "I expected this to do something different"
    - "The wording confused me"
    - "I wish this feature existed"

TESTING TIPS
-------------
  > Think of yourself as a real co-parent using this day-to-day.
    The more realistic your usage, the better our testing.

  > Test at different times -- morning, evening, weekends.
    Some bugs only show up at certain times.

  > Use different devices -- desktop, phone, tablet if you can.

  > Don't fix it yourself. If something breaks, report it exactly
    as it happened.

  > Screenshot everything. A picture is worth a thousand words
    in bug reports.

  > Coordinate with Miguel Lewis! Many features need both
    parents to test.
